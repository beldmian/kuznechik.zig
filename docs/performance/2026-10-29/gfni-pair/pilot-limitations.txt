The initial run.sh used executable names that collided with the narrow/ and
control/ source directories. The first bench pilot completed, then linking
narrow failed. Fixing the names to bin-* and rerunning overwrote that initial
11-pair bench pilot log. Its printed medians were approximately 0.933 encrypt /
0.966 decrypt for dependent pairs and 0.829 / 0.821 for independent pairs.
Those initial raw samples are unavailable and are excluded from final claims.
All pilot logs from the successful rerun, both alternate designs, broad native
repetitions, baseline controls, portable controls, and exact retained-source
repetitions are kept in the evidence archive. No observations are filtered for
noise; reports use matched per-pair ratios, never independent minima.
